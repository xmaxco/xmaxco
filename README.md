### Hi, I'm xmax 🌑

I build products that make noisy markets explain themselves.

Right now that is **[Exlipse](https://github.com/xmaxco/exlipse)** — when a meme token runs, everyone has a story.
Exlipse keeps the record instead: every move gets ranked hypotheses with **dated evidence**, a **confidence level**
and a **base rate**. No ratings, no calls, no guessed intent.

I ship the whole thing end to end — the ingestion pipeline and the why engine in **Go**, the social layer in
**Python**, every piece of evidence in **PostgreSQL**, the app in **React 19**, the visuals in **WebGL**.
Even the launch film is code: 27 shader scenes, era-specific typography and a score synthesized from scratch.
